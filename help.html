Frequently Asked Questions

How does the QR code system work?

Our system generates unique, time-sensitive QR codes for each session. Students scan these using their smartphones, and their location is verified to ensure they are physically present in class.

What if a student doesn't have a smartphone?

Teachers have the ability to manually mark attendance for students who may have technical issues or do not have access to a smartphone during the session.

Is the data secure?

Yes, all attendance data is encrypted and stored securely. We prioritize privacy and ensure that only authorized personnel have access to student records.
